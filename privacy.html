Check Register Pro Privacy Policy

Effective: November 22, 2026

This policy explains how the iOS app Check Register Pro (the "app") handles your information. The app is published by Ninja Rabbit Ventures LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("we," "us").

Short version: We do not collect, see, sell, or share your data. What you enter stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account managed by Apple. We run no server for the app and receive nothing from it.

What the app collects

We do not collect any personal data through the app. The app has no accounts with us, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party software kits, and it does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

Check Register Pro is not a bank. It does not connect to or log in to your bank, credit card, or other financial accounts. You type in your own accounts, balances, transactions, bills, and settings.

How your data is stored and used

  • On your device. Your register is stored on your iPhone or iPad using iOS data protection (Complete Protection), so the file can't be read while your device is locked. It is used only to show your register, balances, forecasts, card payment plans, and reports inside the app.
  • Your own private iCloud. If "Back up & sync with iCloud" is on, a copy of your register is stored in your own private iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit service, so it can be restored after a reinstall and stays the same on your devices signed in to the same Apple Account. We cannot access it. Turning this off stops syncing on that device; it doesn't delete your iCloud copy.
  • Device backups. If you use iCloud Backup or computer backups, the app's data is included in your own backup, which is covered by Apple's terms. We cannot access your backups.
  • Notifications. Low-balance alerts, card due-date alerts, and statement reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is used. They are off until you turn them on.
  • Face ID. Locking the app with Face ID is optional. iOS handles it; your biometric data never reaches the app or us. The app only receives a yes-or-no result.
  • Exports. If you export a spreadsheet (CSV), a full backup (JSON), or a statement or report (PDF or CSV), the file is made on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes is your choice, and it is then covered by that destination's terms. Exported files are not encrypted by the app, so keep them somewhere safe. We never receive a copy.

Household sharing

With a Check Register Pro subscription, you can share your register with one other person you invite, using Apple's iCloud sharing. The shared register lives in the owner's iCloud and is shared through Apple. The person you invite can see and change it until you stop sharing or they leave. Apple may show participants' names from their Apple Accounts so you can see who is in the household. We cannot see the shared register or who is in it. Only invite people you trust with your financial information.

Third parties

The only third party involved is Apple:

  • iCloud / CloudKit stores your synced and shared register in your own iCloud account.
  • The App Store / StoreKit handles downloads and the Check Register Pro subscription. Apple processes payment; we never receive your card number or payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple on your device.

Apple's handling of this information is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy. We do not sell or share your information with anyone.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. To delete it, open the app and go to Settings > Delete All Data. This:

  • deletes the register on that device and clears its scheduled alerts and reminders;
  • deletes your register's copy in your iCloud, which also stops any household sharing you own (the other person keeps their own copy);
  • if you are a household member rather than the owner, removes you from the household; the owner's shared register isn't deleted.

Your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account keep their copy and may put it back in iCloud, so delete there too, or turn off iCloud for the app on them first. You can also manage or delete the app's iCloud data in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud. Deleting the app removes its data from that device. Copies may remain in your existing device backups until those are replaced or deleted.

Because we don't collect personal data, we have nothing to access, correct, or delete on our side.
