Frequently asked questions

What is the Check Register Pro subscription?

It's an optional subscription, available monthly or yearly, that lets you share your register with one other person in your household through iCloud. One subscription covers two people; the subscriber is the household owner. The price is shown in the app and on the App Store before you subscribe.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. New subscribers get a 1-month free trial. After the trial, the subscription renews automatically at the price shown unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the trial or current period. Payment is charged to your Apple Account.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, choose Check Register Pro, and change or cancel it. You can also tap Manage subscription in the app's subscription settings. Apple's steps: Cancel a subscription from Apple. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

On a new device or after reinstalling, sign in with the same Apple Account, open the app's subscription settings, and tap Restore purchases.

How do refunds work?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds directly.

Where is my data, and how does sync work?

Your register is stored on your device. With Back up & sync with iCloud turned on, a copy is kept in your own private iCloud account, so it comes back after a reinstall and stays the same on your devices signed in to the same Apple Account. We can't see your data. You can also export a spreadsheet or a full backup from Settings.

How does household sharing work?

With the Check Register Pro subscription and iCloud sync on, the owner invites one person through iCloud sharing. That person can see and change the shared register until the owner stops sharing or they leave. If the owner's subscription ends, sharing stops and the other person keeps a read-only copy they can view, export, or delete.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings > Delete All Data. It deletes the register on that device and its copy in your iCloud. See the Privacy Policy for details, including households and other devices.

Does the app connect to my bank?

No. Check Register Pro is not a bank and doesn't link to bank or card accounts. You enter your own transactions.
